Golden Lab

Understanding the neural mechanisms and circuits guiding motivated social behavior, with a special interest in aggression reward, addiction, and depression.

The lab is NOT currently accepting applications for postdocs, lab manager/research technician positions, UW graduate students, or UW undergraduates.

Research Focus

Appetitive Aggression

Aggression is often comorbid with neuropsychiatric diseases, including drug addiction, depression, and PTSD. We examine how appetitive aggression mimics addiction symptomatology through dysregulation of reward circuits.

Addiction

We examine the roles of both pro- and anti-social behaviors in addiction processes, focusing on maladaptive appetitive aggression during withdrawal and relapse.

Depression and PTSD

We investigate the common and unique mechanisms behind inappropriate aggression as a diagnostic symptom, aiming to identify novel therapeutic approaches.

Methods

Chemogenetics, optogenetics, calcium imaging, whole-mount light-sheet fluorescent microscopy, and machine-learning approaches.
