Current Application Status

The lab is NOT currently accepting applications for postdocs, lab manager/research technician positions, UW graduate students, or UW undergraduates.

"A lab is first and foremost about the people in it." We seek to foster a scientific community where diverse ideas and opinions are the norm, and we support career development across academia, industry, or other paths.

Postdoctoral Associates and Fellows

Email sagolden@uw.edu with a cover letter describing your research background, your proposed future direction at the Golden Lab, your career goals, your CV, and contact information for three references.

Lab Manager / Research Technician

Submit a cover letter explaining your interest in lab work and career goals, plus a CV with three references. Experience with mouse colonies and a two-plus-year commitment are prioritized.

UW Graduate Students

Contact the director directly to schedule a discussion. Enthusiasm and curiosity are valued over prior computational experience.

Undergraduates

Email a CV, a cover letter detailing your interest in the lab and your goals, any prior experience, and your weekly time commitment.
